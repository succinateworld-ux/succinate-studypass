A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What can I use for free?+

All nine starter lessons, twelve practice questions with explanations, unlimited retries, personal notes, a study planner, progress tracking, and backup export/restore. Open My learning to start. There is no checkout, account, or wallet requirement for these tools.

Is this ready for real payments?+

This is an interactive hackathon UI prototype. Checkout is simulated, prices are illustrative, and no ZEC is sent or payment verified. Production requires merchant wallet integration, server-side payment verification, and protected resource delivery.

Do I need a wallet to try it?+

No. The free lessons, practice, notes, planner, and progress tools work without a wallet. The separate StudyPass checkout is a simulation; future real payments would require a compatible wallet.

Does private mean nobody sees anything?+

No. Shielded transactions can hide details from the public chain. The recipient knows what they receive, and a merchant may link a payment to an order. Hosting and network services may also observe metadata. Production privacy depends on the complete payment and application design.

Are these official examination resources?+

The catalog is an illustrative SuccinateWorld product concept. It does not imply endorsement by NMCN, NCSBN, or any examination body. No exam outcomes or pass rates are promised.
